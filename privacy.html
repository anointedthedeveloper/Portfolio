Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

calendar_today Last updated: July 13, 2026

1. Who We Are

This is the personal portfolio website of Anointed Agunloye, a Full Stack Developer and Lead Software Engineer based in Nigeria. The website is hosted at this domain and serves to showcase projects, skills, and experience to potential clients and employers.

For any privacy-related questions, contact: contact@anobyte.online

2. What Information We Collect

This website collects the following information only when you voluntarily submit the contact form:

  • Full name — so I know who I'm speaking with.
  • Email address — to reply to your message.
  • Phone number — optional, if provided for call/WhatsApp contact.
  • Subject and message content — the inquiry you send.
  • Engagement type — the project/role category you select.

No personal data is collected from visitors who simply browse this site without submitting the form. This website does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. No third-party tracking scripts are embedded.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or project request.
  • Discuss potential collaboration, employment, or freelance engagements.
  • Follow up if you've requested it.

Your information will not be sold, shared with third parties, used for marketing, or added to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

4. Data Storage and Security

Contact form submissions are sent directly via email and are stored only in the email inbox of Anointed Agunloye. No database, CRM, or third-party form service stores your data on this site's behalf.

Reasonable measures are taken to keep your information secure, including using HTTPS for all data transmission. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal data held about you.
  • Ask for your personal data to be corrected or deleted.
  • Withdraw consent for further communication at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@anobyte.online with your request. All requests will be handled within 30 days.

6. External Links

This website contains links to external sites such as GitHub, Dev.to, Vercel, and peterharvardschools.cloud. These external sites have their own privacy policies and Anointed Agunloye is not responsible for their content or data practices.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information through this site, please contact us immediately at contact@anobyte.online so it can be removed.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of this website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
