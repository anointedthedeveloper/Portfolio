Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026.

Who runs this site

This is the personal portfolio of Anointed Agunloye, a software engineer based in Nigeria. Questions about privacy: anointedthedeveloper@gmail.com.

What this site stores

  • No analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies.
  • Your browser stores a theme preference (theme) and a cached copy of public GitHub statistics (gh-cache-v1) in local storage. Neither leaves your device and you can clear them at any time.

Contact form

The form does not send data to a server. When you submit it, your own email app opens with your message pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you send that email. If you do, I use your name, email address and message only to reply, and I do not share them or add you to any mailing list.

Third-party requests

  • GitHub API: your browser requests public profile and repository data from api.github.com to display live statistics. GitHub receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.
  • External links (GitHub, Dev.to, live project demos) are governed by those sites’ own policies.

Your rights

You can ask me to share, correct or delete any email correspondence we have had by writing to anointedthedeveloper@gmail.com. I will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.
